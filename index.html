01 / Human–robot interaction

Robot Brain: Perception to Action

How can a robot turn human cues into the right response in real time?

I developed face and gesture recognition alongside robot-control models, and combined visual cues with spoken commands from multiple users. The behavior-selection logic connected those cues to robot hand movements and corresponding expressions on the robot’s facial screen.

I also implemented control transitions for sitting and stair ascent, then tested recognition and action selection in simulation and on robots. Failure cases informed adjustments to recognition and control settings.

Computer visionMultimodal inputBehavior selectionRobot control

Problem

Human instructions arrive through different signals. The robot needs to interpret them and select a consistent physical response.

My contribution

  • Trained face and gesture recognition models.
  • Connected visual and voice cues to behavior selection.
  • Implemented hand-motion, display, sitting, and stair transitions.

How I checked it

Tested the perception-to-action flow in simulation and on robots, investigated recognition and control failures, and refined the settings based on those cases.

02 / Navigation & safety

SLAM Navigation and Dynamic Obstacle Avoidance

What should the robot do when a planned route is no longer safe?

I integrated SLAM mapping and localization with waypoint navigation and global path planning. Robot pose estimates and obstacle detections informed the next navigation decision.

When a moving obstacle blocked the planned route, I developed logic to switch to local replanning and update the motion goal. I also added a person-aware stop at roughly 0.5 m, then tested stopping and replanning in simulation and on robots.

SLAMLocalizationPath planningDynamic obstaclesSafety stop

Problem

A global route may cease to be usable when people or other obstacles move through the robot’s path.

My contribution

  • Connected SLAM pose estimates to waypoint and global path planning.
  • Implemented route-following versus local-replanning decisions.
  • Added a person-aware stopping rule around 0.5 m.

How I checked it

Ran simulation and physical-robot tests for stopping and replanning decisions; inspected failure cases and tuned localization, planning, and safety parameters.

What these projects taught me

Connect the full loop

Make safety observable
